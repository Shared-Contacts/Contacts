For the My Life app. Nothing here changes the app.

Test 1: your phone's Clock app

Can a web page open the Clock app with an alarm filled in? If it can, the My Life app could set real alarms without a separate app.

Test 2: ringing with the screen off

Can this page keep ringing when it isn't on screen?

  1. Turn the volume up with the side button while this page is showing (it uses the media volume, the one for music and videos).
  2. Tap one of the buttons below.
  3. Press the power button to switch the screen off, and put the phone down.
  4. When it rings, wake the phone and tap Stop.

Try 2 minutes first. The longer ones show whether the phone's battery saving stops it after a while.
