Shared Contacts

Everything you need to get signed in and set up. Takes about two minutes.

The address

Contacts https://shared-contacts.github.io/Contacts/
Your email address and password are sent to you separately. You don't need to create an account — one has been made for you.

1. Sign in

Open the address above, type in your email and password, and tap Sign in. You'll land on the list of contacts that have been shared with you.

If nothing happens when you tap Sign in, check the password for a stray space at the end — that's the usual culprit when copying and pasting.

2. Put it on your phone

It works in a browser, but it's much better added to your home screen. It then opens in its own window and picks up where you left off, instead of starting a new browser tab each time.

Android

Open the address in Chrome, tap the three dots at the top right, and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

iPhone or iPad

Open the address in Safari, tap the Share button at the bottom, scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.

Sign in once inside the installed app and it will remember you.

3. Finding your way around

The search box at the top looks at names, previous names, nicknames, boat names, towns, companies, phone numbers, pets and notes — so half a detail is usually enough to find someone.

The button in the green bar switches between three views:

  • Grouped — by group, then country, then alphabetical.
  • A–Z — one list of everyone.
  • Birthdays — whose birthday is next.

The red + button at the bottom right adds someone new. Tick at least one group before saving.

Menu in the green bar has fuller instructions under How to use this.

4. What's yours and what's shared

Contacts you add are private to you. Nobody else sees them unless you choose to share them, using the Sharing section at the bottom of a contact.

Contacts shared with you work the other way round: you can see and edit them, but you can't delete them, and you file them under your own groups. How you organise your list is nobody else's business but yours.

If something looks wrong

  • Close the app completely and open it again — it reloads everything on start.
  • A blank map usually means a map server is busy. Use the layers button in the corner of the map to switch.
  • Changes someone else makes appear next time you open the app, not instantly.
  • A contact you can't find may simply be one that hasn't been shared with you.

Your password is yours alone — please don't pass it on. If someone else needs access, an account can be made for them.
